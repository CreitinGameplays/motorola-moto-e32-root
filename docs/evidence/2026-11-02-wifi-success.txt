## What the WiFi bring-up took (three gates)

1. **Kernel module** - the SC2355 combo WLAN driver ported in-tree
   (`drivers/net/wireless/sprd_wlan/`, from the mu300-linux-maintained
   Unisoc 2021-2022 GPL drop, ~25 compile fixes for 4.14; full list in
   docs/17). Builds as `sprdwl_ng.ko` (see gate 3).
2. **DT compatible** - the phone's DT advertises `sprd,sc2355-wifi`
   (generic), mu300's table only had `-pcie/-sdio/-sipc` variants; added
   the generic compatible mapping to the SDIO variant and routed
   `SPRD_HW_SC2355_SDIO` through `sc2355_sdio_probe()` in
   `sprd_wlan_probe` (mu300 only ever routed PCIe).
3. **The vendor HAL contract** (libwifi-hal.so, reversed with capstone):
   `is_wifi_driver_loaded()` requires BOTH
   - `property_get("wlan.driver.status") == "ok"` - set by our
     post-fs-data after insmod, AND
   - a `/proc/modules` line starting with `sprdwl_ng` - satisfied by
     naming the module `sprdwl_ng` (Kbuild `obj-m := sprdwl_ng.o`),
     exactly like the vendor's module.
   Otherwise the HAL runs `finit_module("/mnt/vendor/socko/sprdwl_ng.ko")`
   (fails, Exec format error) and sets the property to "unloaded".
