================================================

   and /data was wiped once:

     ro.boot.flash.locked        = 0
     ro.boot.verifiedbootstate   = orange
     ro.boot.veritymode          = enforcing
     sys.boot_completed          = 1

   (adb shell getprop, captured after the first unlocked boot and again
   after a further reboot.)

     avb_slot_verify() save slot_data to *out_data | [00005573]

     androidboot.vbmeta.avb_version=1.1 androidboot.vbmeta.device_state=unlocked

   The same partition contains no 'LOCK FLAG'/'SKIP VERIFY' line, so that
   banner only reaches the early/serial console; the device_state argument

   "VerifiedBoot-UNLOCK" with the chip-unique VerifiedBoot-HUK-DERIVE key):

     f5 53 44 ab 11 63 dd 2a 21 53 5f 77 db a1 7c 54
     23 aa ad 81 5e dd be 88 42 fe e7 f2 f5 d0 71 76

   Deterministic: identical across boots, scratch pages and module loads.

   /dev/block/by-name/miscdata at offset 0x2000 are byte-identical
   (artifacts/marker.bin == artifacts/marker_readback.bin).
